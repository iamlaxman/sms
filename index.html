01 / Purpose

Why we built this adda.

The purpose of SMS Adda is to understand how vulnerable and how easy it is to detect faults and weak points inside automated SMS systems. During this research, we identified several of these weak points across different platforms and services — and we document them here so defenders, developers, and students can learn from them.

01 / VULNERABILITY

How systems break

Small oversights in queue handling, rate limiting, and validation turn into systemic weaknesses. We map them, one gateway at a time.

02 / DETECTION

How easy it is to spot

Many of these weak points are trivially observable from the outside. That visibility is itself a finding worth documenting.

03 / DISCLOSURE

What we do with it

We publish the pattern, not the exploit. The goal is defensive literacy, not a weaponised tool.
