Eligibility

To use SMS Adda you must:

SMS Adda delivers to Nepali (+977) numbers only. We do not currently support international destinations.

06

Message delivery

We route your message to the best available gateway, but final delivery depends on the recipient's mobile network. SMS Adda cannot guarantee:

  • Exact delivery time — most messages arrive in seconds, but delays of up to several minutes can occur.
  • Delivery to numbers that are switched off, out of coverage, or on Do-Not-Disturb lists.
  • Delivery when a recipient's carrier blocks bulk or transactional SMS.

A successful API response means the message was accepted by the gateway, not that it was delivered to the handset. Refunds for undelivered messages are at our discretion and typically only apply to confirmed gateway-side failures.
