02 How it works

Three steps.
Then you're in.

  1. 01

    Install or open

    On mobile, tap "Download App" to add SMS Adda to your home screen. On desktop, click "Continue on Web" to launch instantly.

  2. 02

    Read the disclaimer

    SMS Adda is a research playground. Everything here is educational. Before you interact with any gateway, you acknowledge the terms.

    Start exploring

    Browse the six mapped gateways. Study the traffic. Learn what makes these systems break — and how to defend them.
