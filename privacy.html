01

Overview

SMS Adda ("we", "us", "the service") is an OTP delivery tool for Nepali mobile numbers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

We've written this in plain language on purpose. If you have questions after reading it, the contact section at the bottom tells you how to reach us.

Short version

We collect the minimum data needed to run the service. We never sell your data. Recipients' phone numbers are passed through to the SMS gateway in memory and are not stored long-term.

02

Information we collect

We collect three categories of information, and only what's needed:

Category What Why
Account data Your first name, surname, and phone number. Timestamp of last sign-in. To identify you, sign you in, and let you send messages.
Usage data Timestamps, number of messages sent, chosen gateway, success/failure responses. To operate the service, debug issues, and prevent abuse.
Technical data Browser type, device type, IP address (transient, for rate limiting only). To secure the service and stop spam.

We do not collect: your contacts, your location, your messages (except the metadata listed above), or any third-party data.

03

How we use information

We use the data we collect for the following purposes:

  • Providing the service — signing you in, routing messages to gateways, showing you the delivery status.
  • Preventing abuse — rate limiting, blocking spam, and detecting automated misuse.
  • Debugging — understanding failures and fixing them.
  • Legal compliance — responding to lawful requests from Nepali authorities.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties. Ever.

04

Recipient data

The phone numbers you send messages to are treated with the highest level of care. Here's exactly what happens:

  • Your browser sends the numbers to our worker in the request body.
  • The worker validates the format and passes them to the SMS gateway.
  • The gateway delivers the message and returns a success/failure flag.
  • The numbers are held in memory only for the duration of the request — typically a few seconds.
Key point

Recipient phone numbers are not written to disk, not stored in a database, not logged beyond the transient request, and not sold or shared with anyone outside the delivery pipeline.

This means if you send 100 messages today, we have no way to tell you tomorrow who you sent them to.

05

Cookies & local storage

SMS Adda is deliberately light on tracking. We don't use advertising cookies, third-party analytics, or fingerprinting scripts.

  • Session storage — a single entry storing your name and phone number so the app remembers you between pages. This is stored in your browser's localStorage and never sent to us beyond what's required to sign you in.
  • No cookies — we don't set any HTTP cookies.
  • No third-party trackers — no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no ad networks.

You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data for SMS Adda.

06

Sharing & disclosure

We share your data with as few parties as possible. In practice, only two categories of recipients ever see any data:

  • SMS gateways — the phone number you're sending to is passed to whichever gateway delivers the message. These gateways are third-party services with their own privacy policies.
  • Cloud infrastructure — our workers run on Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes requests on our behalf but does not access the content beyond what's technically required.

We will disclose data to law enforcement only when legally compelled to do so by a valid order from a competent authority in Nepal. Where possible and lawful, we will notify you of such requests.

Important

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party. Period.

07

Data retention

We keep data for as short a time as possible:

  • Account data — retained as long as your account exists. Delete your account at any time and it's removed within 30 days.
  • Usage metadata — retained for up to 90 days for debugging and abuse prevention, then automatically purged.
  • Recipient phone numbers — held in memory for the duration of the request only (seconds). Never persisted.
  • Rate-limit counters — retained for up to 24 hours, keyed by phone number, then deleted.
08

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data:

  • All traffic to and from SMS Adda is encrypted with HTTPS / TLS.
  • Our infrastructure runs on Cloudflare's edge network, which provides DDoS protection and redundant routing.
  • Rate limiting and abuse detection prevent unauthorised bulk usage.
  • Access to internal systems is restricted to the operator (Laxman Poudel) and any explicitly authorised staff.

No service is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

10

Children's privacy

SMS Adda is not intended for anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
